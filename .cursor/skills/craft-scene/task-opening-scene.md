# Task: Craft the Opening Scene

## Status

**Ready.** The creative producer starts here. No other scene work begins until the opening is done.

## What to craft

The first thing the player sees in Episode 1. Sofi and Pluto arrive at VIRAC, deep in a Latvian forest, at dusk.

The current vision says: "Sofi saw aliens in a cartoon and wants to find one. She and Pluto arrive at a big telescope deep in a forest."

The opening is:

- What Sofi sees when she first arrives
- What the telescope looks like from the outside
- What Pluto is doing
- Whether they talk (what Sofi says first)
- The weather, the light, the mood
- Whether the door is open or Sofi has to find a way in
- What pulls Sofi forward into the building

## Before you start

Read these documents (do not edit them):

- `docs/game-design/vision.md` — general decisions (especially: Pluto is the interface; no text-only exposition; no HUD; Sofi's impatience respected; feel the loneliness first, then curiosity)
- `docs/game-design/world.md` — Sofi's and Pluto's proposed sample lines (use them, or ask the human to rewrite them)
- `docs/game-design/art-direction.md` — dead state palette, camera at 1.0 m, scale conventions
- `docs/game-design/places.md` — Episode 1 = VIRAC, Latvia. Real facts: forest, remote, former Soviet site, RT-32 dish. The building is real; the interior and the story are ours.
- `corpus/viraclatvia/facts.md` — currently empty. Research VIRAC on the web. Find: what the site looks like from the approach road, what the main building looks like, what the forest is like, what the weather is like in autumn.
- `corpus/viraclatvia/fiction-boundaries.md` — currently empty. Decide what is invented and write it here as a table.

## Questions to discuss with the human first

Do not write a single word of the scene until you have the human's answers to these:

1. **What does Sofi see first?** The gate? The road? The dish silhouette against the sky? The building?
2. **What is Pluto doing right now?** Walking beside her? Flowing ahead? Perched somewhere?
3. **Is Sofi talking? What does she say first?** (Use her voice from `world.md` or ask the human for a new line.)
4. **Is the door open, locked, or missing?** This decides the first thing Sofi has to do.
5. **What is the weather?** Dusk? Rain? Mist? Clear sky with first stars?
6. **What does the observatory look like from the outside?** Real VIRAC reference or invented? Both?
7. **What pulls Sofi forward?** A sound? A light? Pluto pointing? The cartoon in her head?

## What to produce

A document `docs/game-design/episode-01/scenes/opening.md` following the scene template in the craft-scene SKILL.md. Or, if the human prefers, edit the scene directly into `docs/game-design/episode-01/GDD.md` as a new section before the existing spaces.

The output must include:

- "What Sofi feels" — using the human's words verbatim
- "The real place" — a real fact about VIRAC from web research, cited
- "What is invented" — clearly stated
- "Entry", "The discovery" (first learning beat: dark and light? counting?), "The change", "Out"

## After you finish

The human approves. Then the next task is the first interior scene (probably the entrance hall or the generator shed). The creative producer continues crafting scenes in order until the whole episode is described.

## Important

- Do not show Pluto's origin.
- Do not name the Voice.
- Do not show the alien.
- Do not skip the human discussion step. This is a collaboration, not a solo assignment.
