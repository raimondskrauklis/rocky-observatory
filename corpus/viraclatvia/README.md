# VIRAC — Irbene, Latvia

Ventspils International Radio Astronomy Centre. A 32-metre fully steerable radio telescope (RT-32) and a 16-metre one (RT-16) hidden in pine forest near the Baltic coast, about 30 km north of Ventspils, by the Irbe river. Built 1967–1973 by the Soviet military as a secret station to intercept Western satellite communications. Its code name was **Zvyozdochka** — "Little Star". When the Russian army withdrew in 1993–94 they poured acid in the motors, cut the cables, and took the documentation. Latvian scientists took the site over on 22 July 1994 and, over two decades, brought the big dish back to life. It is a working scientific instrument today. Beside it stands the ghost town that housed 2,000 people — apartment blocks, a school, a kindergarten — now being reclaimed by forest.

Episode 1 of *The Lost Observatory* is set here. In the game world the site is dead and waiting; in reality it is alive. See `fiction-boundaries.md`.
